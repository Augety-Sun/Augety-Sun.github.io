About Me

Learn more about my academic background and contact information

I am Chenhao Sun, with research interests in remote sensing image intelligent processing and disaster comprehensive assessment. During my master's studies, I conducted in-depth research on remote sensing image change detection, anomaly detection, and intelligent decision-making, participating in multiple national defense key R&D projects and industry-academia collaboration initiatives.

As first author or student first author, I have published multiple papers in top-tier remote sensing journals such as IEEE TGRS and IEEE GRSL, and hold an invention patent. I also focus on practical applications, having interned at the Yangtze River Commission to deploy change detection technology for building monitoring in the Yangtze River Basin.

I am currently dedicated to exploring cutting-edge technologies such as multimodal semantic guidance and self-supervised learning for remote sensing applications. Academic exchange and collaboration are always welcome.
